MARKETING GUIDELINES

1. Marketing content must be clear, concise, and professional.

2. Do not invent product specifications.

3. Do not make unsupported medical, performance, or technical claims.

4. Marketing content should focus on genuine customer benefits.

5. The target audience should influence the tone and messaging.

6. Include a clear call to action when generating a campaign.

7. Avoid misleading or exaggerated claims.

8. Use only information available in the approved product knowledge base.
